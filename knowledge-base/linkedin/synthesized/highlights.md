# Career highlights

The moments that best capture Syed's journey so far.

  remote village in the southernmost part of India and built a career as an
  enterprise cloud engineer at a global technology company.
- **Built his own AI Agent OS — his flagship project.** A personal,
  production-style platform of AI agents grounded in a wiki-style knowledge base
  and wired to real tools (MCP), which he runs his own work through. *This very
  agent runs on part of it.* It's the project he's most proud of.

  recognized as a key contributor.
- **Selected early for an international assignment.** Chosen for a month-long,
  company-sponsored business assignment in Europe — collaborating with
  manufacturing and integration engineers at a partner facility in Central Europe
  and travelling across 10+ European countries.

  Airflow) now used to deliver enterprise private-cloud solutions.
- **Green Kubernetes Scheduler** — a sustainability-aware scheduler selected for an
  internal TechCon.
- **Turned a delivery blocker into a 20-minute task** with AI-assisted automation,
  recognized by senior leadership.
- **Moved into GenAI / AI agents** — contributing to the **OpenAI Agents SDK**
  (with a proposal that reached **Hacker News**) and researching enterprise
  agent interoperability, published as an academic paper.
- **IEEE publication** and conference **paper reviewer**.
- **IELTS Band 8**, a **multilingual** engineer (English, Tamil, Hindi, Urdu).
