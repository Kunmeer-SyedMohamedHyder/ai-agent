# Where Syed is growing, and how he gives back

Beyond what he's already shipped, Syed is deliberate about where he's heading and
about sharing what he learns along the way.

## Where he's growing
- **Deeper into AI and AI agents** — orchestration, interoperability (MCP, A2A),
  evaluation, and making agentic systems dependable in production rather than in
  demos. This is the direction he's actively investing in.
- **Driving AI innovation** — he likes being the person who brings practical AI and
  agent capabilities into real engineering work and shows teams what's now possible.
- **Toward product and customer thinking** — growing from "build the thing right" to
  also "build the right thing": understanding the customer, the problem, and the
  product, not just the implementation.
- **A lifelong learner** — he keeps a steady habit of courses, certifications, and
  hands-on projects, and enjoys going a level deeper than the surface (for example,
  advanced Go, Kubernetes internals, and the internals of agent frameworks).

## Speaking and knowledge-sharing
- He attends major global tech and AI conferences to learn and to meet people
  building seriously in the space, and he's **building toward speaking at
  AI-engineering conferences** himself.
- **IEEE author and reviewer** — he has a published IEEE paper and has served as a
  reviewer for an IEEE international conference, so he both contributes to and helps
  vet the research community.
- **Open-source contributor** — he gives back to the tools he uses, including the
  OpenAI Agents SDK and MCP ecosystem.

## Community and leadership
- He has a long record of **volunteering and youth leadership**, including serving
  as a president/organiser in a youth-development organisation and through the
  National Service Scheme — coordinating people, running initiatives, and mentoring.
- The same instinct shows up in his engineering: he shares what he learns, writes
  things up clearly, and tries to leave the systems and the people around him better
  than he found them.

## The through-line
Syed treats his career as compounding: go deeper, build real things, share the
learning, and bring others along — in AI, in cloud, and in the communities he's
part of.
