# FlightAI – Multilingual Voice-Enabled Flight Booking Assistant

**🗓️ Duration:** Jul 2025 – Jul 2025

**📄 Description:**

Developed a smart AI-powered chatbot using Gradio and OpenAI's language models to assist users with flight bookings. FlightAI supports multilingual interaction, allowing users to choose a preferred language for both input and output. The assistant offers voice-based communication, with options for spoken responses in selected languages. The chatbot enhances accessibility by displaying side-by-side English and translated chats, providing a seamless user experience for diverse audiences.
