# 🤝 Universal Solidarity Movement of Value Education for Peace

- **Role:** Student Volunteer
- **Cause:** Education
- **Duration:** Sep 2015 to Sep 2015

**Description:**
As a student volunteer with USM, I had the opportunity to participate in an outreach program at an orphanage. During this activity, we engaged with the children through meaningful interactions, building connections, and fostering a positive environment. We also focused on raising awareness about important social issues, contributing to the well-being and development of the children. This experience not only allowed me to give back to the community but also deepened my understanding of empathy, teamwork, and social responsibility.
