### **Automated Meta-Learning Frameworks for Dynamic Neural Architecture**
- **🗓️ Published On:** March 20, 2025  
- **📝 Description:**  
  This publication explores the integration of meta-learning techniques with automated neural architecture search to enable adaptive and efficient model design. It proposes a dynamic framework that optimizes neural networks based on task-specific requirements, reducing manual intervention and improving generalization across diverse datasets. Published with IEEE, the work highlights advancements in automation within machine learning workflows and their impact on scalable AI development.
